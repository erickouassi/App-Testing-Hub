What is App Testing Hub?

App Testing Hub is a simple, developer‑friendly platform that helps Android users discover open and closed beta testing opportunities. Developers publish a small XML feed, and the platform automatically pulls their testing information into a clean, mobile‑friendly directory.

Why This Platform Exists

Many developers struggle to recruit testers, and many users never hear about exciting new apps until they’re already released. App Testing Hub bridges that gap:

  • Developers get visibility for their beta programs
  • Testers get early access to new apps
  • Everyone benefits from a clean, ad‑supported, privacy‑respecting experience

How It Works

Developers host a small RSS feed (usually on GitHub). Once approved, their feed is added to feeds.json. The platform automatically:

  • Fetches each feed
  • Parses app metadata
  • Calculates testing progress
  • Displays the app in the directory

Users can browse apps, join Google Groups, open testing links, save favorites, and track their joined or completed tests — all stored locally on their device.

What Data We Store

App Testing Hub does not collect personal data. Your activity (saved apps, favorites, joined tests) is stored only in your browser’s local storage.

Who Built This?

App Testing Hub is created and maintained by Eric Kouassi. The goal is to support developers, testers, and the Android community with a clean, accessible, and transparent testing directory.

Developers can follow the Developer Guidelines to create a valid feed and request approval.
